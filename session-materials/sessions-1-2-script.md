# Rehearsal script — sessions 1 and 2

Practice script for piloting the sessions. It follows the session kit and the sheets in `session-materials/materials.pdf`. Manuel is a character. In a real session, keep the structure and replace his lines with what the co-designer says.

**Who is in the room**

- **Henrique** — facilitator. He leads. One person asks at a time.
- **João** — the other interviewer. Notes, recorder, materials, and in session 2 the voice of the phone. He asks when Henrique gives him the turn.
- **Manuel** — 80, Benfica, widower since 2019 (Lurdes). Former electrician. Lives alone, second floor. Daughter **Ana**, 58, Oeiras, married to Rui. Son **Paulo**, 54, Maia. Sister **Rosa**, 76, Amadora, who also refuses the smartphone. Granddaughter **Catarina**, 31, and her partner Tiago: their son **Martim** is seven weeks old. Grandson **Nuno**, 33: his daughter **Madalena** is two. Friend **Joaquim** at the café.

**The two phones**

- A Nokia with buttons, years old. Ana’s number is saved as `ANA`. This is the phone he actually uses.
- A Samsung Ana bought him in July so he could be in the family group. It lives in the sideboard drawer. Screen protector half peeled. PIN `1946` on a post-it in Ana’s handwriting. He does not know how to work it.
- On the fridge: one printed photo of Martim in the first week, already out of date. The new photos are only in the WhatsApp group **Família**.

**Place.** His kitchen, afternoon, good light. Portuguese deck on the table, the seven of copas bent. Coffee in the pot. The Nokia by the bread bin. The Samsung is not out yet.

**How they speak.** One at a time. Plain words: telemóvel, botões, mensagens, grupo. Nobody says “app”. Session 1 does not mention a cover, a button prototype, or any idea of the team’s. They offer to write. A missed tap is a design problem.

---

## Session 1 — Explore the problem

About 70 minutes. Bisca is the welcome, so the hour runs long. They let him finish the hand.

**In the bag until each activity.** Declaration, two copies, large type. Communication map, page 2, printed A3. Green and red dots. Thick marker. Recorder, off.
**Do not bring out.** Action cards, templates, mock-ups, storyboard.

### Arrival — he will not start with questions

*(Doorbell. He is already sitting, deck in the middle, two cups poured, a third for himself. Coats on the chair. He does not get up fast.)*

**Manuel.** Come in. Shoes wherever you want, the floor has seen worse. You are Henrique, and you are João. Ana described you badly, but I can count to two. Sit. The coffee is mine, so it is strong.

**Henrique.** Obrigado por nos receber, senhor Manuel. We are from Técnico. Before anything, I should say what this is, and there is a paper—

**Manuel.** After. If we start with the paper, the coffee dies and you ask me ten things at once. In this house we play first. Bisca. You know bisca, or only sueca from the faculty?

**João.** My grandfather played bisca. I know it a bit. Sueca I know better.

**Manuel.** Then you will try to follow suit and you will be wrong. Bisca is not sueca. Three cards, you play what you have, trunfo eats the rest. My house, I deal. João, do not shuffle my deck. The seven of copas is bent and I like it bent.

**Henrique.** We can play. João, the recorder stays off.

**João.** Off.

**Manuel.** Good. You talk too, both of you. I am not playing against a wall. I just do not want the interrogation before I know if you can hold a card.

### 0–16 min — Bisca

*(He deals three cards each, clockwise, one at a time. He turns the next card. It is the five of ouros.)*

**Manuel.** Trunfo: ouros. A five. No points in the trump and the suit is poor. Still, ouros win. Henrique, you are on my right, you lead. João, you are last, which is a gift. Do not waste it.

**Henrique.** I play the rei de copas.

**João.** Four of copas. I should follow, no?

**Manuel.** You do not have to follow. You already did, so the four is dead. I have no copas.

*(He lays the two of ouros, slowly, so they see the suit.)*

**Manuel.** Two of ouros. Trunfo. I take your rei. It is only four points, Henrique, do not make the face. The lesson is: do not lead a rei while I am smiling.

**Henrique.** I thought the rei was strong.

**Manuel.** Strong if nobody has trunfo. Everybody has trunfo today, the five is staring at you. Again.

*(They draw back to three. Manuel draws first because he took the trick. He is neat about the order and he makes João put the won cards face down in a separate pile.)*

**Manuel.** Your pile, your points. Do not mix them with the hand or at the end we fight, and I am too old to fight over a two.

**João.** What do you do when you are not teaching students?

**Manuel.** I go to the café and I take Joaquim’s money at this same game. I eat. I water the plants. On Sunday I go to Ana’s, if she does not change the hour. Your lead, João. You won nothing, Henrique leads next. No. I took it, so I lead.

*(He leads the ás de espadas. Henrique throws a six. João throws a valete and then touches it, wanting it back.)*

**Manuel.** Leave it. A card played is played. Your valete is three points and it is mine. You do not pull it back. In the phone you can pull things back. In bisca, no.

**Henrique.** You still play with your sister?

**Manuel.** Rosa plays sueca and she cheats at the counting. She lives in Amadora. We talk on the phone, the normal phone, the one with buttons. She is like me. The family bought her a smartphone too. She told them to take it back or she would put it in the soup. They leave her alone because she shouts. I do not shout. So they keep trying.

*(He says this while collecting the trick. He does not look up. João almost asks more. Manuel taps the deck.)*

**Manuel.** Draw. We are not in the soup yet.

*(Two more tricks. Henrique trumps a dama and is pleased. Manuel nods, once.)*

**Manuel.** There. You learned. Four points to you. João, you kept the seven of paus. That is ten. You are not asleep.

**João.** My grandfather said the seven is the bisca. Eleven for the ás, ten for the seven, four the rei, three the valete, two the dama. The rest are nothing.

**Manuel.** Your grandfather was right. The little cards are for losing with style. Last tricks, the deck is finished, no more drawing. Play what you have.

*(They finish the hand. Manuel turns the piles over and counts out loud, pairing, fast. He does not need a pen.)*

**Manuel.** João, thirty-one. Henrique, twenty-two. I have the rest, sixty-seven. The deck is one hundred and twenty. I win. You are not disgraced. Henrique, you gave me the rei and you recovered. João, you sit on the seven too long. A seven in the pocket is zero.

**Henrique.** One more hand?

**Manuel.** One more, short, and then you may take out your paper. Deal is João’s. And while he deals, I will say the thing Ana sent you here for, so it is not a surprise later. They want me to use a smartphone. I have one. It is in the drawer. I do not know how to work it. They want it because of the group, the one where they all talk at the same time. There is a baby. I will tell you properly after the hand. Deal, João.

*(João deals. Trump is the ás of paus, which Manuel whistles at.)*

**Manuel.** Ás of paus as trunfo. The best card is stuck on the table until the end. Annoying. This is how the smartphone feels. The thing you want is under a glass and the rule is stupid.

*(They play four tricks, quicker. The Nokia rings. A plain ringtone, loud. The screen says ANA in big letters. Manuel puts his cards face down with a coin on top.)*

**Manuel.** Do not look at my cards. And do not touch the phone.

*(He answers on the speaker by habit, then takes it off speaker when he hears her.)*

**Manuel.** Ana. I am here. The boys are here. We are playing. … No. I have not opened it. … Sunday is fine, at one, if you do not write another hour and forget to call. … He is well? Good. Kiss him. I have to go, I have an ás on the table and these two will cheat.

*(He hangs up. He is smiling and a bit embarrassed.)*

**Manuel.** She asks every time. “Did you open it.” The baby is Martim. Seven weeks. Her daughter Catarina’s boy. She asks if he is well as if I would know better than she does. I know what she tells me on this phone. The rest is in the other phone, and I do not go there.

**João.** Shall we finish the hand?

**Manuel.** Yes. You are learning. Henrique, your card, and stop looking at the Nokia as if it is going to confess.

*(They finish. João wins the second hand by four points because the ás of paus came to him at the end. Manuel is satisfied, not annoyed.)*

**Manuel.** Good. The table is yours. Put the deck aside, do not lose the bent seven. Now. What is this paper.

### 16–23 min — Consent, after the game

**Henrique.** We are students at Técnico. The project is about how people use the phone to talk to family. You are the co-designer with us. It is not a test of you. Three conversations of about an hour, here, at a time you choose. You can skip any part. You can stop. This sheet says that, and it says what we do with notes and recordings. May I read the consent part? It is long. I will go slowly.

**Manuel.** Read. I have my glasses. I will stop you if you use a word from the university.

*(Henrique reads. He translates the stiff sentences into plain Portuguese as he goes: notes, photographs, a name instead of his full name, deleting the recording, stopping whenever he wants. João does not interrupt. Manuel stops him twice.)*

**Manuel.** Photos of my face, no. I am not a poster. My hands, the phones, the paper we write, yes. The fridge, the photo of the baby that is already there, yes, that photo is not a secret. The recorder, yes, if it stays with you. I do not want my voice in the cloud. Ana has tried to explain the cloud. I told her clouds are for rain.

**Henrique.** The recorder stays with us. We delete it on the date written here. João is the one who runs it. Only one of us will ask at a time. If we both talk, tell us.

**Manuel.** I will tell you. In bisca you already talk over each other. Start the machine. The name for the work is Manuel. It is my name and it is half of Benfica. My surname stays out.

**João.** Starting now.

*(João says the date and “session one, kitchen” into the recorder. He opens the notebook. He does not hide it.)*

### 23–45 min — Stories

**Henrique.** Conte-me a última vez que usou o telemóvel para falar com alguém. Como foi?

**Manuel.** You just saw it. Ana. The Nokia. Her name comes up, `ANA`, big, because she typed it herself in capital letters. I press the green button. I hear her. I hang up with the red one. That phone I know. I had a phone like it when Lurdes was alive. The buttons do not move when someone at the shop decides to improve my life.

**Henrique.** What did she want, besides Sunday?

**Manuel.** She wanted to know if I had seen Martim. Not seen him here. Seen him in the group. I said no. She did the sigh. You know the sigh. Then she told me he slept four hours, which Catarina wrote to everyone except, in practice, to me. I said “good, kiss him.” That is our conversation now. She reports the group to me by voice, like a translator. I am the foreigner in my own family.

**João.** Henrique, may I ask one?

**Henrique.** Go on.

**João.** When she does that sigh, what do you do with the phone after you hang up?

**Manuel.** I put it back by the bread. I look at the fridge. Come, you can look, it is two steps.

*(They look. One photo, A5, held by a magnet from a pharmacy. Martim, red, eyes shut, hospital blanket. Ana’s handwriting on the white border: “Martim, primeira semana.”)*

**Manuel.** This is the baby I have. Ana printed it because she was tired of me saying I had not seen him. She says he already has another face. More hair. The new pictures are in the group, every day, sometimes ten. Catarina is a good mother and a terrible photographer, or the opposite, I would not know. Tiago sends them at midnight. Nuno sends Madalena saying words. Madalena is two. She is Nuno’s girl, my other great-grandchild. I have held Madalena. She says avô when she feels like it. Nuno says she said it into the phone, a voice message, “for avô”. I have not heard it. He told me on a normal call, laughing. I laughed. Then I went to the kitchen and I was not laughing.

*(He sits back down. He pours more coffee. His hand is steady enough. He pushes the sugar toward João and not toward himself.)*

**Henrique.** Lembra-se de uma vez em que o telemóvel lhe complicou a vida?

**Manuel.** Which phone. That is already the problem. You say “the phone” as if I have one.

**Henrique.** The time that cost you the most.

**Manuel.** Two times. I will tell both. You have an hour, you said an hour, so do not rush me to the second before the first is finished.

Last month I went to the health centre for the blood test at ten, which is the hour written in my diary. The lady said no, it was eleven, it had been changed. Ana had written it in the group. “Pai, amanhã às onze, não às dez.” She wrote Pai in a conversation with twelve people, and she thought that was telling me. I stood there with the paper from the doctor and I felt like a fool. I called her from the street, from the Nokia, from the paper list in my wallet in case the `ANA` button failed. She said “but I wrote it.” She did write it. I did not read it. I cannot read it. It is inside a phone I do not know how to open.

The other time is worse and smaller. Sunday lunch, three weeks ago. They put the smartphone on the table between the rice and my glass. Catarina had come with Martim, the one time I saw him in a person and not on paper. He was asleep. I held him for a minute and Rui was hovering as if I would drop him, so I gave him back. Then Ana said “Pai, now you will learn, so you can see him every day.” Four people watching. Catarina said “just put your finger here.” The screen moved. A line of faces. I pressed where she said and a film of the baby started, with sound, and also my own face in a small square, which I did not ask for. I did not know how to stop it. Nuno laughed, not badly, the way family laughs. I put the phone down. I said I had to check the car. I do not have a car anymore. I went to the balcony. We did not do the lesson. The phone came home in Ana’s bag and then she brought it back the next week and put it in that drawer. “For when you are ready.” I am eighty. Ready is a word for people with time to look stupid in private.

**Henrique.** Como se sentiu? On the balcony.

**Manuel.** Hot in the face. Angry at myself more than at them. They are not cruel. They want the baby to have a grandfather who answers. Rosa would have put the phone in the soup. I stood on the balcony and I could hear them inside showing each other the same pictures I was supposed to learn. That is the part I do not like to say. The family is in the next room, and also inside a machine I cannot open, and I am on the balcony of both.

**Henrique.** Quando precisa de ajuda com o telemóvel, a quem pede? O que costuma pedir?

**Manuel.** Ana. Only Ana. Paulo is in Maia. He is kinder about it. He says “Pai, when you want, I will sit with you, no audience.” Then he forgets, or the baby photos happen on a Tuesday and he is at work. I will not ask Catarina. She has a seven-week child and she does not need a project. I will not ask Nuno. He laughs, and I love him, and I do not want to be his joke.

What I ask Ana for is small. “Tell Catarina he is beautiful.” “Tell them I am coming on Sunday at one.” “Read me what they decided about the christening.” She does it, and then she says “it would be easier if you just looked.” She is right. It would be easier for her. It is not easier for me. For me, looking is the hard part.

**João.** What do you ask her to do with her fingers, exactly, when she is here?

**Manuel.** “Open the group. Make the photo big. Tell me who sent it. Close it before I touch something.” Sometimes: “Write ‘sim’ to Ana so she stops asking.” I do not want her to write a speech from me. I would not speak a speech. I would say: he is beautiful, I saw, thank you.

**Henrique.** Já recebeu alguma chamada ou mensagem que lhe pareceu estranha? O que fez?

**Manuel.** On the Nokia. A message, not a call. “Olá pai, parti o telemóvel, este número é novo, preciso de um dinheiro.” It said pai. Paulo’s name was not on it. I do not read fast, but I can read one message on that phone because it is one message, large enough, and it stays still. I did not reply. I called Ana from the wallet paper, not from any number inside the message. She said it is the trick, the burla, they do it to parents, delete it. I did not delete it. I do not trust delete. What if delete sends it? It is still in the Nokia. I can show you later if you can look at it without fixing it. I do not want it fixed. I want it left.

Since that day I trust the group even less. If a stranger can say pai on the simple phone, what can they do on the other one, where I cannot even see who is talking? Catarina posted the baby’s face for the whole group. I understand why a mother does that. I also understand that I do not know who is in that group. Ana says it is only family. I have not seen the list. I would not recognise the list if I saw it.

**Henrique.** Porquê? Why does the list worry you if Ana made it?

**Manuel.** Because I am the person who almost answered a false pai. I do not get to be casual about it. And because there are cousins in that group I would not give a key to the house. A photo of Martim is not a key, Ana says. Maybe. I still want to know the name before I see the child. Is that strange?

**Henrique.** It is not strange.

**Manuel.** Good. Write that down, João, since you are writing everything else. He wants the name before the baby. The rest of your notes I assume are my failures.

**João.** I am writing what you do, not a grade. You won the first hand by a lot.

**Manuel.** That is the correct use of a notebook.

*(He gets up, opens the window a little, sits again. A minute where nobody asks a question. The recorder keeps running. João writes “silence, he opened the window, not uncomfortable, just done with that story.”)*

**Henrique.** Is there a recent time the phone, either phone, actually helped?

**Manuel.** This morning. Paulo called on the Nokia. He does not text me. He knows. He said Madalena has a cold and they are not coming down this month, and he said Martim, according to Ana, looks like Lurdes’s father, which is a lie families tell, the child looks like a potato. It helped because it was a call, his name on the screen, `PAULO`, and I could ask “is the girl very ill?” and hear the answer. No photo. I did not need a photo of a cold. I needed the sentence. You see the difference. A sentence I can take by ear. A baby I need to see. They are not the same job, and the family has put both jobs in the same place, the group, and then they are surprised I do not enter.

### 45–58 min — Phone tour

**Henrique.** Pode mostrar-me como liga à Ana?

**Manuel.** On the phone I use. Yes. The other one, we will see if it has battery. I am not promising a performance.

*(He takes the Nokia. Right hand, thumb on the green key, left hand bracing the bottom. Glasses on. He does not hesitate. He opens the contacts, `ANA` is first because she named it to sort at the top. He shows them the screen, then cancels before the call goes out.)*

**Manuel.** Like that. Three presses. If `ANA` were not saved I would use the paper in the wallet. I will not type a number from memory. I get one digit wrong and I call a stranger, and then we are in the burla story again. The buttons are big enough. My hand shakes late in the day, not now. At night I prefer to call from the landline. Yes, I still have one. Ana laughs at it. It does not laugh at me.

**Henrique.** E como vê se alguém lhe mandou mensagem? On this phone first.

*(He opens the message inbox. One thread is the burla, still there, from a mobile number with no name. He holds the phone so they can see and not take it.)*

**Manuel.** This is the one I do not delete. The others are Ana, rare, she knows I do not read them, and the operator, which I ignore. On this phone, a message is a nuisance, not my life. My life, according to my daughter, is now here.

*(He goes to the sideboard. The drawer sticks. Under envelopes and a bulb he does not throw away, the Samsung, face down, charger cable tangled. He brings both to the table. The screen protector is lifting at the corner. He tries to power it on. It shows a battery empty icon and dies.)*

**Manuel.** You see. It is dead because a phone that does nothing is a phone I do not charge. The Nokia is charged because I use it. If I charge this one I am admitting I will learn it. Plug it in. The socket is there. I am not getting on the floor.

*(João plugs it in. They wait. Manuel talks while they wait, which is what people do.)*

**Manuel.** She paid for it. Not a fortune, she said, do not worry. I do worry. A thing that costs money and sits in a drawer is a reproach. Every Sunday it is in the room with us even when it is here. “Did you charge it.” I would rather she had spent the money on the christening cake.

*(The phone vibrates on the table, loud, a pile of delayed notifications. He flinches.)*

**Manuel.** It is possessed. Make it stop. I did not touch it.

**João.** It is catching up. The messages arrived while it was dead. I will not open them. Do you want to hold it, or leave it on the table?

**Manuel.** On the table. My fingers do things I do not order.

**Henrique.** The paper in the drawer, the small one. May we use what is written on it? We will not read it aloud into the recording if it is a code.

**Manuel.** It is my birth year. 1946. Ana wrote it. She said “you will not forget this.” I forget where she wrote it, which is the same thing. Type it, João, you have thinner fingers. And say what you are doing while you do it.

**João.** I am waking the screen. I am typing one, nine, four, six. I am not opening anything else. It is in. The screen is a grid of little pictures.

**Henrique.** Pode mostrar-me o grupo? Only if you want. You can point. We do not have to enter.

**Manuel.** I want to see if Martim is there. I do not want you to tour the house. The green picture. Ana said the group is inside the green picture. WhatsApp. I can say the word. I am not a child. I do not know what to press after the word.

*(He points at the green icon. His fingernail taps twice, too short, nothing happens. The third tap opens it. A list. At the top, Família, a baby as the small round photo, and a grey badge with 128.)*

**Manuel.** One hundred and twenty-eight what. Minutes? People? Sins?

**Henrique.** It is the number of messages you have not opened. You do not have to open them.

**Manuel.** If I do not open them, Catarina thinks I do not care about her son. If I open them, I will press the wrong thing and buy a crib. Open it. You press, I watch. No. I press. If I watch you, I learn nothing and you learn that I am decorative.

*(He taps the group. The thread jumps to the bottom, which he does not expect. A large blur of a recent photo loads: a baby in a yellow shirt, awake, on Tiago’s arm. Under it, Catarina: “hoje já sorriu”. Above it, more photos, voice notes, Ana’s “que lindo o nosso menino”. He leans in. Glasses. About thirty centimetres. He does not scroll.)*

**Manuel.** Is this Martim? This one is older than the fridge. He is smiling. The fridge one does not smile. So this is new. Why is it at the bottom? The beginning should be at the top. Books are at the top.

**João.** On this phone the newest is at the bottom. The first days are up above, if you move the list that way.

**Manuel.** Who invented that. A person who does not read books. So for weeks I have been… I have not been in here at all. But if I had come in, I would have read it backwards and thought the hospital was today. That is a design by a sadist.

*(He tries to make the smiling photo bigger. He pinches in, the way people do when they mean “smaller” without knowing. The text shrinks. He stops. He looks at his fingers as if they misbehaved.)*

**Manuel.** I broke the letters. Put them back. Do not laugh.

**Henrique.** You did not break it. The page changed size. João can put the size back, and he will say each step. Or we can leave it. Your choice.

**Manuel.** Put it back. I want the smiling one big, if that is possible without a ceremony.

*(João puts the pinch back, narrating. He asks Manuel to tap the photo once. Manuel taps the text by mistake. The keyboard opens. He pulls his hand back as if from a pan.)*

**Manuel.** I am not writing. Close that. I will write “sim” to the president by accident.

*(João closes the keyboard. Manuel taps the photo, a clean tap this time, because his hand is resting on the table and only the finger moves. The photo fills the screen. Martim, yellow shirt, eyes open. The room is quiet.)*

**Manuel.** There. That is a grandson’s son. He looks like Catarina when she was born, which means he looks like a potato with Ana’s mouth. Leave it like this for a moment. Do not scroll. I am looking.

*(They let him look. Maybe twenty seconds. He nods. He does not know how to go back. He presses the side of the phone. The screen goes black. He thinks he turned it off forever.)*

**Manuel.** I killed it. Ana will kill me.

**Henrique.** You only put it to sleep. The button on the side does that. Press the same one again.

*(He does. The lock screen. The PIN again. He remembers the four numbers this time and is grimly proud, and types them himself, slowly. He does not find the group again without hunting. He locks the phone on purpose and puts it face down.)*

**Henrique.** Há alguma coisa no telemóvel em que nunca toca? Porquê?

**Manuel.** On the Nokia, nothing is frightening except delete, and the message I told you about. On this one, I do not touch the shop, the camera if I can help it, anything with a euro sign, and video. At lunch the video started by itself, or by my finger, which is the same. I will not have my face in the corner while Martim is crying. Also I never touch the thing that sends the voice. Madalena’s message is one of those. I would like to hear it. I do not know which picture is the ear.

**Henrique.** O que gostava que o telemóvel fizesse e não faz?

**Manuel.** I want this Samsung to show me today’s Martim, big, and to say in a voice whose photo it is and what Catarina wrote, one sentence. “Catarina says he smiled today.” Then I want to say “tell her he is beautiful” without the keyboard. And if Ana writes that the blood test is at eleven, I want that in the same voice, not lost under ten photos of a yellow shirt. I do not want to learn the grid. The family wants me to get a smartphone. They already got it. They did not get me a way to work it. Rosa could refuse. I cannot refuse, because the refusal includes the baby.

*(Note, not said to him: right-hand thumb, left hand bracing the Nokia; Samsung only felt safe flat on the table; missed the first taps; pinch made text smaller; keyboard opened by accident; side button read as “off”; newest-at-the-bottom was a real surprise; he could type 1946 on the second try; he stayed on one photo once it was big.)*

### 58–68 min — Communication map

*(João clears the cups to one side, not the phones. He lays out page 2 of the materials, the A3 “Com quem fala?”, rings already printed. Green and red dots. Marker. Manuel looks at “Eu” in the centre.)*

**Henrique.** Com quem fala mais? Como: chamada, mensagem, videochamada, visita? You point, we write, big letters. If a person does not belong, we do not write them.

**Manuel.** Write in Portuguese. And do not write the whole of Ana’s building. The group is not twelve people to me. It is one door I cannot open.

*(João writes. Manuel moves two notes with his finger after they are down. He places the dots himself. He is particular about Joaquim.)*

### Session 2 debrief — same day, 30 minutes

1. **What we learned**
   - The job is today’s photo of Martim, with the sender’s name first, the sentence read aloud, and a short reply in his words. “I have a Nokia. It does not contain my great-grandson.”
   - He chose the full cover on the Samsung Ana already bought. Bumper still shows the icons. The box is a third phone.
   - He will abandon it if calling Ana is harder than the Nokia, or if a burla can show a baby, or if his own face appears.
2. **What surprised us.** He charged the phone before they arrived. Video moved from a refusal to a conditional Gostava. He asked for silence while he “looks”. He wanted the name repeated even after he had just heard it.
3. **What we had wrong.** One button. He needs red to end, or video and calls are not safe for him. A chatty opening line. Replacing the Nokia as an opening move. Treating family photos as a secondary feature. They are the reason he will not do what Rosa did.
4. **Methods.** The mats worked because torch, weather, radio, and magnifier were there to refuse. The radio refusal is about Lurdes, and they almost missed that by rushing Não preciso. Blank cards became requirements, not decorations. Holding objects to his ear, the table, and the pocket decided the form. João as the voice was right. Henrique asking and João not adding charm was right. The last hand of bisca was not method. It was manners. It also showed he was tired and still himself.
5. **Change for the prototype.** One window, one photo, no carousel. Name, then sentence, then wait. Confirm before any send or call. Unknown numbers: no photo, offer Ana, no payment, no link. Green and red only. Dark plain cover, ear and speaker open, charge light. Wizard-of-Oz for the voice. Test the yellow-shirt story, one burla, one “ligar à Ana”, and one misunderstood “Paulo” that ends by doing nothing.

## Where the two hours land

**Problem he signed.** Manuel needs a way to see the new photos of Martim, to hear Madalena and the family group, and to reply with a short sentence, without learning the smartphone. The group is where the baby is shown and where news is written. He needs the sender’s name first, because a message that says olá pai may be a burla.

**What he chose.** A plain dark cover on the Samsung Ana already bought. Large window, one photo, no list, no 128. Green button to talk, small red button to end. The voice says the name before the picture and before the sentence, in European Portuguese, short, then it waits while he looks. He can answer only “ele é lindo”, “sim”, or “liga-me”, and it repeats his words before sending. People Ana did not save never get a photo on the screen. The Nokia stays until calling Ana is as easy as the green button he already trusts. It is not an emergency pendant, not a radio, not a class in how to use WhatsApp.
